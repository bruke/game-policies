本政策说明 CuteTigerStudio（“我们”）如何处理您使用《一刻时钟》（Yike Clock，以下简称“本应用”）时的信息。本应用提供时钟显示、外观与显示设置以及番茄钟计时功能。

1. 我们处理的信息

本应用不要求创建账号，不含广告或第三方分析、追踪 SDK。我们不通过本应用收集或向我们自己的服务器传输个人信息、使用情况数据或设备标识符。本应用不访问通讯录、照片、相机、麦克风或精确位置。

为提供应用功能，本应用会读取设备当前的本地时间，并根据您的设置在设备上运行计时器。设备时间不会发送给我们。

2. 设备本地保存

本应用会在设备应用存储中保存您选择的时钟外观、显示偏好、番茄钟设置及当前计时状态，以便下次打开时继续使用。上述信息由应用保存在本机，不会由我们上传或同步到服务器。

本地设置会在您保留应用数据期间保存。您可以通过系统设置清除本应用的数据，或卸载应用来删除设备上的本地数据。Android 或设备操作系统提供的备份、恢复功能可能按照您的系统设置处理应用数据；这些系统服务不由我们运营。

3. 信息共享与第三方服务

本应用不出售、出租或与第三方共享您的个人信息。应用本身不集成广告、分析或崩溃报告 SDK。Google Play、Apple App Store 或设备操作系统可能根据其自身服务处理下载、更新、诊断或备份所需的信息；这些处理由相应服务提供方按照其政策负责。

4. 数据安全、保留与删除

应用设置保存在设备应用存储中，并受设备操作系统提供的应用隔离和访问控制保护。我们不持有本应用的用户设置或计时记录副本。删除应用数据或卸载应用通常会删除本地保存的信息；系统备份中的副本（如有）由相应操作系统或备份服务管理。

5. 儿童隐私

本应用不以收集个人信息为目的，也不要求用户提供个人信息或创建账号。家长或监护人如对儿童使用本应用有疑问，请通过下方邮箱联系我们。

6. 政策变更

如果本应用的数据处理方式或功能发生变化，我们会更新本页面并修改更新日期。重大变更将在适用情况下通过应用内或其他适当方式告知。

Yike Clock Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026
Developer: CuteTigerStudio
Contact: boxiaohu@gmail.com

This policy explains how CuteTigerStudio (“we”) handles information when you use Yike Clock (“the app”), which provides clock display, appearance and display settings, and a Pomodoro timer.

1. Information We Process

The app does not require an account and contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. We do not collect or transmit personal information, usage data, or device identifiers through the app to our own servers. The app does not access contacts, photos, the camera, the microphone, or precise location.

The app reads your device’s local time to display the clock and run the timer. Device time is not sent to us.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores your clock appearance, display preferences, Pomodoro settings, and current timer state in the app’s storage on your device so they are available when you use the app again. We do not upload or sync these settings to our servers.

These settings remain stored while you keep the app’s data. You can delete them by clearing the app’s data in system settings or uninstalling the app. Android or your device’s operating system may handle app data through backup and restore features according to your system settings; these services are not operated by us.

3. Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. Google Play, the Apple App Store, or your device operating system may process information needed for their own services, such as downloads, updates, diagnostics, or backups, under their respective policies.

4. Security, Retention, and Deletion

App settings are stored in the app’s storage on your device and protected by the app isolation and access controls provided by the operating system. We do not hold copies of your app settings or timer records. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app generally deletes locally stored information. Any copies in system backups, if enabled, are managed by the relevant operating system or backup service.

5. Children’s Privacy

The app is not designed to collect personal information and does not ask users to provide personal information or create an account. Parents or guardians with questions about a child’s use of the app may contact us using the email below.

6. Changes to This Policy

If the app’s features or data practices change, we will update this page and its revision date. Where appropriate, we will notify users of material changes through the app or other suitable means.
