Tempolet 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年11月6日
开发者：CuteTigerStudio
联系邮箱：boxiaohu@gmail.com

本政策适用于 CuteTigerStudio（“我们”）提供的 Tempolet Android 版。应用无需创建账号或登录，不包含广告、使用情况分析或追踪 SDK。

1. 音乐访问与播放

您选择扫描设备音乐时，应用会请求 Android 的音频读取权限。Android 12 及更早版本使用系统提供的存储读取权限来扫描音频。您也可以通过系统文件选择器授权特定文件或文件夹，应用会保留相应的读取授权，以便之后继续播放或重新扫描。应用读取音频及其文件名、歌曲、艺人、专辑、封面、时长等信息，用于建立音乐库、搜索、播放及显示媒体控件。应用不上传、修改或删除原始音乐文件。

2. 本地数据、备份与删除

应用在设备的应用存储中保存音乐来源与索引、收藏、歌单、播放队列和进度、最近播放、听歌统计、语言及外观偏好、反馈草稿等信息。听歌统计包含曲目信息、播放时间、收听时长和次数。我们不运营云同步服务，也不会将这些本地记录上传到我们的服务器。

这些信息会在您保留应用数据期间保存。您可以在应用设置中关闭后续听歌统计或清空已有统计，在音乐来源页移除导入来源，或通过 Android 设置清除应用数据。卸载通常会删除设备上的应用数据，原始音乐文件不受影响。根据您的设备和系统设置，Android 的备份或迁移功能可能复制应用设置、音乐库索引、听歌记录及反馈草稿。系统备份中的副本由相应系统服务管理。

3. 您主动发送反馈时

反馈按钮会在您的邮件应用中预填收件人、主题和您填写的反馈正文。您确认发送后，我们会收到发件邮箱和邮件内容。若您勾选附带设备信息，正文还会包含应用版本、构建号、设备厂商和型号、Android 版本。应用生成的反馈正文不附带音乐库或听歌记录。

我们将反馈邮件用于回复咨询、排查问题和处理隐私请求。邮件由您使用的邮件服务和我们的邮件服务提供方处理。我们在处理相关事项所需期间保留邮件，法律要求继续保留的情况除外。您可以通过上方邮箱请求删除相关邮件。

4. 政策网页与其他系统服务

本网页由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 可能为安全等目的记录 IP 地址等访问数据。Google Play 和 Android 也可能按其各自政策处理应用下载、更新或系统备份等服务所需的信息。GitHub 隐私声明 · Google 隐私政策

5. 政策更新与联系方式

如果应用功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页和更新日期。有关隐私、邮件删除或其他数据处理问题，请联系 boxiaohu@gmail.com。

Tempolet Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026
Developer: CuteTigerStudio
Contact: boxiaohu@gmail.com

This policy applies to Tempolet for Android, provided by CuteTigerStudio (“we”). The app requires no account or sign-in and contains no advertising, usage analytics, or tracking SDKs.

1. Music access and playback

When you choose to scan for music, the app requests Android audio access. On Android 12 and earlier, it uses the system’s storage read permission to scan for audio. You can also grant access to specific files or folders through the system file picker. The app retains those read grants so it can play or rescan those sources later. It reads audio and related information such as file names, titles, artists, albums, artwork, and duration to build your library and provide search, playback, and media controls. The app does not upload, modify, or delete your original music files.

2. Local data, backups, and deletion

The app stores music sources and library indexes, favorites, playlists, the playback queue and position, recently played tracks, listening statistics, language and appearance preferences, and feedback drafts in app storage on your device. Listening statistics include track information, playback times, listening duration, and play counts. We do not operate a cloud sync service or upload these records to our servers.

This information stays in app storage while you keep the app’s data. In the app’s settings, you can turn off future listening statistics or clear existing statistics. You can also remove sources in the app or clear its data in Android settings. Uninstalling generally removes the app’s local data and leaves the original music files intact. Depending on your device and system settings, Android backup or device transfer may copy app preferences, library indexes, listening records, and feedback drafts. The relevant system service manages any backup copies.

3. Feedback you choose to send

The feedback button opens your email app with the recipient, subject, and feedback text filled in. If you send the email, we receive your sender address and the message. If you select the option to include device information, the message also includes the app version and build number, device manufacturer and model, and Android version. The generated message does not attach your music library or listening history.

We use feedback emails to respond to inquiries, investigate problems, and handle privacy requests. Your email provider and ours process the message. We retain the correspondence for as long as needed to handle the matter, unless the law requires longer retention. You can request deletion of the correspondence using the email address above.

4. This policy page and system services

GitHub Pages hosts this page. GitHub may log visitor IP addresses and other access data for purposes including security. Google Play and Android may process information for downloads, updates, or system backups under their own policies. GitHub Privacy Statement · Google Privacy Policy

5. Changes and contact

If the app’s features or data practices change, we will update this page and its revision date. For privacy questions, email deletion requests, or other questions about data handling, contact boxiaohu@gmail.com.
